# ALMOTASEMBELLAH AWWAD — Brand Kit v1

**AI Engineer & Digital Product Builder**

I turn complex AI into useful, distinctive digital products.

أحوّل تقنيات الذكاء الاصطناعي المعقدة إلى منتجات رقمية مفيدة ومميزة.

Arab-first startups, founders and product teams; international technology teams seeking applied AI, product engineering and distinctive digital experiences.

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon | `#0B0B0B` | Primary background and text |
| Bone | `#EAE7DF` | Primary light surface and text |
| Signal | `#C65D32` | One controlled accent |
| Graphite | `#8E8B83` | Secondary information |

- Display type: condensed or neo-grotesk sans serif, bold and oversized.
- Body type: neutral sans serif with generous leading.
- Metadata: monospace, uppercase, small and precise.
- Grid: visible editorial columns, asymmetric but aligned.
- Imagery: real product interfaces, system diagrams and deliberate monochrome photography.

## Voice

- Precise, calm and evidence-led.
- Explain the problem, decision and result.
- Prefer “I built” and measurable facts over hype.
- Arabic first when addressing the Arab market; English version always available.

## Avoid

- Blue-purple AI gradients, glassmorphism and generic SaaS cards.
- Excess badges, animated typing effects and vanity counters.
- Calling unfinished prototypes complete products.
- Using `®` unless the mark is officially registered.

## Content rhythm

- Build note: one technical decision from a real project.
- Product note: problem → decision → result.
- Visual note: before/after with the design rationale.
- Development log: honest progress on VERVE.

Every post should prove one of the three brand pillars.
